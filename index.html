Privacy Policy

For the LostMyAccent Discord bots · Last updated: October 29, 2026

This policy describes how the LostMyAccent Discord bots (including the Ops, Production & Sourcing, Wholesale, and Marketing bots) handle information when used in a Discord server.

Information processed

To respond to commands, a bot may process the command input and the Discord information needed to handle it, such as your Discord user ID, username, server ID, channel ID, and relevant message or interaction content. This information is processed only to provide the requested bot function.

Storage and retention

The bots do not intentionally save command data or Discord user information to a persistent bot database or files. Information is held in working memory only as needed to handle a command and is then discarded. This statement covers storage by the bot itself; Discord and the bot's hosting infrastructure may handle technical data under their own policies and configurations.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent information processed by the bots. Information is not shared with third parties by the bot, except as necessary for the bot to communicate with Discord and provide the requested function. Discord's handling of data is governed by its Privacy Policy.

Your choices and questions

You can stop using the bots at any time and ask a server administrator to remove a bot from the server. Because the bots do not maintain persistent user-data records, they generally have no stored command history to retrieve or delete. For questions about this policy or the bots' data handling, contact the LostMyAccent server owner or administrator through Discord.

Changes

This policy may be updated if the bots' functionality or data practices change. The latest version will be published at this page, with the updated date shown above.
